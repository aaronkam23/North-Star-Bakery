Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery created to bring customers together through fresh and delicious baked goods.

We believe a bakery should be a welcoming place where families, commuters, and community members can enjoy quality products.

Our Ingredient Philosophy

We believe that quality ingredients are an important part of making delicious baked goods. Our ingredient sourcing practices should reflect our commitment to quality and freshness.
